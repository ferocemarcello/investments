Why the real rate

A nominal return alone assumes prices never rise. If your portfolio grows 6%/yr but inflation runs 2%/yr, your purchasing power only grows about 3.9%/yr. This ledger divides by that real rate, so the withdrawal keeps its buying power forever instead of quietly shrinking.
